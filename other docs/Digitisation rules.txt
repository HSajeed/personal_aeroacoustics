Digitisation rules

BPF = 22 × 7808 / 60 = 2863 Hz. Convert every frequency axis to BPF.
For A1 to A3, mask the tone peaks (about ±3% around each k·BPF) and keep only the broadband envelope.
In B2, the 97% span curve has no tones, so use it whole. At 81% and 25%, digitise only the floor between tones.
Cut the hot-wire variance integral at 20 kHz. The report attributes the roll-off above that to hot-wire frequency response. Run it once truncated and once extrapolated, and report both.
